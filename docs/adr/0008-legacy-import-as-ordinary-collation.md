# A legacy collation imports as an ordinary collation

Each verse of a classic-app TEI export becomes a collation whose alignment is built from a transcription of its base text, with its readings imported as authored and its witnesses as full manual witnesses.

- Scholars arriving with a legacy collation are the ones most likely to keep adding manuscripts, so a later transcription must be placeable into an imported collation.
- The base text's word and gap positions map directly onto alignment columns.
- Rejected: apparatus-only imported collations with no alignment, because they could only ever gain manual witnesses.
